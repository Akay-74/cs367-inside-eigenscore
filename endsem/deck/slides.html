Problem statement

Beyond a Single Layer: Multi-Layer and Cross-Depth EigenScore for Hallucination Detection

LLMs hallucinate confidently

A wrong answer is written in the same fluent, assertive tone as a correct one, so the text alone gives no warning.

No answer key at run time

A deployed detector must flag likely-wrong answers using only the model itself, without the correct answer.

Q: In which year did the bridge open?   Model: “The bridge opened in 1928.”  Confident, fluent, and wrong.

Goal: give every answer a hallucination score, so that unreliable answers can be flagged before they reach the user.

Existing work

EigenScore, our reproduction, and its two blind spots

Sample K = 10 answers, read each answer's last-token state at the middle layer (16 of 32), and score the log-volume of the cloud:

ES = (1/K) · Σ log λᵢ(Σ + αI)

Agreeing answers give a low score; scattered answers flag a hallucination.

Midsem reproduction (250 CoQA questions, 8 GB GPU): EigenScore 83.5 AUROC, best of five methods, matching the paper's ranking.

Problem we address
  • One hand-picked layer. Detection quality changes with depth, and the paper's own ablation shows it.
  • Spread only. The score ignores how the answers are arranged: shuffling or rotating them gives the same number.

Proposed extension

Four training-free extensions, tested on the same samples

  • M1 · Multi-layer EigenScore: average over a fixed band of layers
  • M2 · Normalised EigenScore: equalise dimensions before the covariance
  • M3 · Hybrid: fuse with token-level confidence
  • XDS · Cross-depth stability: new, does the answers' arrangement survive across layers?

Designed to be trustworthy

  • Every score is computed from the same 10 samples, so all comparisons are paired
  • Layer band and tests pre-registered in git before any result
  • One primary test: M1 vs EigenScore

Proposed extension · M1

Multi-layer EigenScore: stop betting on one layer

MLES = mean ES(l), l ∈ 8 … 24
  • The band is the middle half of the network, ⌊L/4⌋ to ⌊3L/4⌋, fixed before any result
  • Each layer gives a noisy reading of the same uncertainty, and averaging reduces the noise
  • Choosing the best layer after seeing results inflates it; a fixed band avoids that bias

Proposed extension · M2 and M3

Normalised and hybrid EigenScore

M2 · Normalisation

A few fixed dimensions in LLM hidden states are orders of magnitude larger than the rest ("massive activations", Sun et al. 2024) and can dominate the covariance.

  • M2a: scale each answer embedding to unit length
  • M2b: standardise each dimension using previous questions only
M3 · Hybrid

EigenScore reads the spread of internal states; LN-Entropy reads word-level confidence. Different signals can make different mistakes.

HYB = ½ [rank(MLES) + rank(LNE)]

Label-free and untuned: equal weights.

Proposed extension · new concept

Cross-depth stability: is the answers' arrangement preserved?

  • At each layer, record which answers resemble which: their 45 pairwise distances
  • Correlate that pattern between consecutive layers (representational similarity analysis)
XDS = 1 − mean corr(Dl, Dl+1)
  • Knows the answer: samples are paraphrases of one fact, so the arrangement is stable and XDS is low
  • Confabulating: nothing anchors the samples, so the arrangement reshuffles and XDS is high
