03 · The math
EigenScore: how spread out are the answers?
10 answer embeddings (projected to 2-D)
Σ = ZTJZ
covariance, 10 × 10
λ1 … λ10
eigenvalues = spread per direction
E = 1K log det(Σ + αI) = 1K Σi log λi
low · trust
high · hallucination
α = 10⁻³ keeps Σ invertible
Σ is only 10 × 10: cheap
Animationdots = the 10 answers; the grid fills in their pairwise covariance
Animationbars = eigenvalues of the covariance, i.e. how much the answers vary in each direction
Animationanswers pull together → every eigenvalue collapses → score moves to LOW
Animationanswers spread apart → eigenvalues grow → score moves to HIGH (likely hallucination)
04 · The blind spot
Confidently wrong? Then the answers agree.
Fix: test-time feature clipping
all wrong but all identical → low score → wrongly trusted
activations of one neuron · penultimate layer
0.2%
99.8%
FC(h) = clip(h, hmin, hmax)
thresholds: 0.2% / 99.8% per neuron
from a memory bank of N = 3000 tokens
→ less overconfidence: hidden errors start to scatter
