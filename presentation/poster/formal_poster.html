LLM Hallucination Detection using Multi-Layer EigenScore

1.Problem Statement

Large Language Models often hallucinate: they give confident answers that are wrong, in the same tone as correct ones.

Goal: for every answer, output a hallucination score that says how likely it is to be wrong, without knowing the correct answer, and running on an 8 GB laptop GPU.

2.Key Idea: Ask Again, Check Agreement

We measure agreement not on the words but on the model’s internal representation of each answer: 4096 numbers read from its hidden layers.

3.Measuring Spread with Eigenvalues

EigenScore = average of log(eigenvalues)
Small eigenvalues → answers agree → low score · Large eigenvalues → answers disagree → high score

4.Worked Example

Toy case: 3 answers, each represented by 2 numbers (real system: 10 answers × 4096 numbers).
