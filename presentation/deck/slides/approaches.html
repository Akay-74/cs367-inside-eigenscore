[Speaker: Aayaan · about 40 s] Existing detectors look in one of two places. The first group uses token probabilities: perplexity, length-normalised entropy and the energy score measure how uncertain the model is about each next token. But being confident about tokens is not the same as being right about meaning. The second group samples several answers and checks whether they agree; lexical similarity, for example, measures word overlap between the answers. This works better, but it compares surface words and discards the dense representation the words came from. INSIDE takes a third route: it reads the model's internal states directly, on the premise that the semantic information is still present inside the network.
