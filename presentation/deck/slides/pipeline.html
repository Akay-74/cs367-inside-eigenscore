[Speaker: Shlok · about 45 s] Thank you, Aayaan. This is the full EigenScore pipeline. For each question, the model is sampled ten times at temperature 0.5. For every sampled answer we take one vector: the hidden state of the last generated token at the middle layer of the network, which for a 7-billion-parameter LLaMA model is a 4096-dimensional vector. The ten vectors form a ten-by-ten covariance matrix, and the EigenScore is the average logarithm of its eigenvalues. The interpretation is simple. If the ten answers mean the same thing, their embeddings cluster tightly and the score is low. If they mean different things, the embeddings spread out, the score is high, and the answer is likely to be a hallucination.
