[Speaker: Shlok · about 25 s] EigenScore has one blind spot. If the model is confidently wrong, it gives the same wrong answer all ten times. The embeddings then coincide, and any consistency-based score, EigenScore included, reads that agreement as a sign of correctness. The paper calls these self-consistent hallucinations, and its second contribution addresses them by reducing overconfidence inside the network.
