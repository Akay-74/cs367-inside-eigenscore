[Speaker: Prapti · about 45 s] These are our main results. Grey bars are the paper's numbers and blue bars are ours. The ordering of the five methods is identical to the paper's: EigenScore is the best detector, followed by lexical similarity, length-normalised entropy and perplexity, while the energy score is barely above chance, which is expected because it was designed for out-of-distribution detection rather than hallucination. Our EigenScore reaches 83.5 AUROC. One detail matters for a fair comparison: the paper's EigenScore of 80.8 already includes feature clipping, so the like-for-like number from our clipped run is 82.5. Despite a different model and 4-bit precision, our absolute values are within a few points of the paper's.
