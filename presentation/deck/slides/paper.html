[Speaker: Aayaan · about 30 s] The paper was published at ICLR 2024 by Chen and colleagues, and the authors released their code on GitHub. It makes two contributions. The first is EigenScore, which measures how much several sampled answers disagree by using the eigenvalues of their embedding covariance. The second is test-time feature clipping, which truncates extreme activations so that answers the model repeats with false confidence can also be detected. The paper evaluates both on three models and four question-answering datasets. I will now hand over to Shlok, who will explain the method.
