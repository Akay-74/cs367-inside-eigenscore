[Speaker: Prapti · about 40 s] These are the ROC curves from our run without clipping; the brown curve, EigenScore, lies above the others over most of the range. To check whether EigenScore's advantage over the strongest baseline, lexical similarity, is real rather than noise, we ran a paired bootstrap over questions with ten thousand resamples. EigenScore is ahead by 2.9 AUROC points, with a 95 percent confidence interval from minus 0.6 to plus 6.5, and it is better in 95 percent of resamples. This agrees in sign and size with the roughly three-point advantage the paper reports on CoQA. With only 250 questions, the interval just touches zero.
