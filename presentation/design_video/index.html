Future Directions
planned · not yet evaluated
01

Multi-layer EigenScore

Combine a band of middle layers into one score, instead of one hand-picked layer.

02

Add token confidence

Join the spread score with the model’s own word-level confidence to catch confident mistakes.

03

Adaptive sampling

Stop early when the first answers clearly agree: fewer than 10 answers, lower cost.

Next steps for the system; these ideas have not been tested yet.
